Help

        
      


      

        
Which mode

        
Ship is for boats and ferries, when you cannot see the horizon.

        
It shows a line that stays level with the horizon. Rest your eyes near it.

        
Car is for passengers in cars, buses and trains who cannot watch the road.

        
It shows dots at the sides that move with each turn and brake.


        
Warnings

        
    
          
  • Never use it while driving.
    • 
          
  • Do not use it on a plane. The line is wrong when the plane turns.
    • 
          
  • If you feel worse, take the glasses off and look far away.
    • 
          
  • 
            This is a proof of concept, not a medical device. It may not help, and it could make
            you feel worse. You use it at your own risk.
          
    • 
        


        
Does it work?

        

          Ship: in studies, a level horizon line reduced seasickness. It did not cure it.
        

        

          Car: the evidence is weaker. If you can look at the road ahead, do that first.
        

        

          Studies: Tal 2012, Hemmerich 2020 (ship). de Winkel 2021 (car). Nobody has tested this
          app itself.
        


        
Tips

        
    
          
  • Look at the real horizon or the road when you can.
    • 
          
  • Face the way you are travelling.
    • 
          
  • Breathe slowly.
    • 
          
  • You can move your head. A quick move makes the picture wobble for a second.

Buttons

        
    
          
  • Set Up: do it once, sitting still, before you set off. It is remembered.
    • 
          
  • Re-level: if the picture sits crooked, keep your head straight for a few seconds, then tap it.
    • 
          
  • Strength: how far the dots move. Car only. The ship line always matches the real horizon.
    • 
        

        
Car mode uses your phone's location to tell the car's movement from your head's.

        
Everything works without phone signal.
